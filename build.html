Technical detail · who can change the recipient

PAR stores one creator-fee recipient per token. Only the current recipient can call transferCreatorFeeRecipient. A wallet recipient can therefore hand the right to a new operator; fees already credited to it stay claimable by that wallet. The holder, burn and floor vaults have no such function, so their choice is fixed for normal operators. What happens to fees inside a vault depends on PAR: holder payouts are sent by PAR’s distributor wallet, burn-vault buybacks are run by PAR’s operator (per PAR’s published source it can delay or misprice a round but not redirect it), and the floor vault’s source is not published (its interface shows operator(), rebalance and assignUnattributed). Separately, PAR runs a Community Takeover (CTO) process: after manual review by PAR (which may decline), the factory owner can move any fee recipient — to a wallet, an X/GitHub creator account or a fee mode — with a 3-day public notice. The token, pool, supply and creator tax never change. So “fixed” and “transferable” describe the normal path, not an absolute guarantee. Pairing creates markets; fee routing is a separate mechanism.

Opening buy · optional

The only buy guaranteed to land first: it executes inside the launch transaction itself, before anyone else can trade. It is public — anyone can see that the deployer bought in the launch transaction, and SyncNet records the amount in your launch intent. Leave it at 0 for no buy.
