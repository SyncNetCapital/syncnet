Join EARLY

Fans pay your wallet directly. SyncNet verifies that the wallet belongs to your YouTube channel and gives each supporter a private proof that they were there.

Creator onboarding is not open on this deployment.

1 · Connect the wallet that receives support

Use a wallet you control and keep. Fans will send directly to it. You can change it later, with a 48-hour waiting period that your current wallet can cancel.

Continuing signs a free message with your wallet, then opens Google to confirm which YouTube channel you control (read-only access, nothing is posted).

3 · Sign your creator manifest

What fans can send you. Minimums are yours to choose. Identity is your channel id; your handle and name can change freely.

The signature comes from the receiving wallet. It is free and creates no transaction.

Change the receiving wallet

Connect the new wallet and sign a new manifest. The change takes effect after 48 hours. Your page shows the pending change, support meanwhile still goes to your current wallet, and your current wallet can cancel it. If you suspect your current wallet is compromised, pause support first.

Money moves directly from fans to your wallet. SyncNet never receives or holds it and takes no fee. Nothing here shows who supports you; supporters keep their receipts private unless they share a card.
