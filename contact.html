Report abuse, legal or privacy issues

Use this section to report content on SyncNet, including a Project Home, a token page, a listing or an image:

  • phishing, scams or fraud;
  • impersonation of a person, project or brand;
  • malicious links or malware;
  • copyright or trademark infringement;
  • privacy violations or doxxing;
  • other unlawful content;
  • privacy requests about your own data.

Send a direct message to @sync_on_par on X. Include the page address (for example /site/0x… or /project/0x…), what is wrong and, for rights claims, which right you hold. Do not post other people’s personal data publicly.

SyncNet reviews reports and may suspend or stop serving content under the Terms. SyncNet cannot remove anything from the blockchain or from third-party IPFS nodes.

Security vulnerabilities

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately, before any public disclosure: send a direct message to @sync_on_par on X and ask for a private reporting channel. Do not open a public GitHub issue for a vulnerability, and never include private keys, seed phrases or other secrets in any report.
