Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026. SyncNet is designed so that the wallet remains under your control. The site does not request or store private keys or seed phrases.

Project drafts and uploaded preview images are handled in your browser. The current public beta does not create a new launch-transaction recovery record because transaction submission is disabled. If your browser already contains a recovery record created by an earlier SyncNet build, the site may read that local record so you can check the status of the existing transaction. Clearing site storage removes that local record.

Blockchain transactions and on-chain token metadata are public. Live market, holder and contract data are requested from third-party RPC, indexer and explorer services. Those services, the hosting provider and linked external sites may receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser information and request metadata under their own policies.

SyncNet does not currently use an account system for the public beta and does not automatically create social accounts or post on your behalf. If analytics, accounts or additional hosted profile services are introduced later, this notice should be updated before those features are enabled.
