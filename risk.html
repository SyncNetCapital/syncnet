Risk disclosure

SyncNet is experimental, non-custodial software. Cryptoassets are volatile and blockchain transactions may be irreversible. You may lose some or all assets used in cryptoasset activity.

Software and market risks

Smart contracts, wallets, networks, RPC services, indexers, explorers and third-party infrastructure can fail, change behavior or report stale information. User-created projects and metadata can be inaccurate, misleading or malicious. A successful browser simulation does not guarantee that a later transaction will be included or execute successfully.

Nothing on SyncNet guarantees value, liquidity, distributions, rewards, returns, users, holders, community or attention. A USDG market does not peg or stabilize a project token.

Connection is not endorsement

Connecting a project to another asset describes a market relationship. It does not imply that the other asset’s team created, approved, endorsed or controls the project.

Simulation and real-launch boundary

The builder can read live eligibility, construct PAR launch calldata, run an eth_call simulation and estimate gas. An operator-authorized test deployment can also request an irreversible launch transaction after additional checks. A mined launch is immediately tradable.

Project images pinned to IPFS become public and effectively permanent. The final ipfs:// URI should be reviewed before launch.

PAR dependency. SyncNet launches tokens through PAR’s contracts on Robinhood Chain, which SyncNet does not control. PAR’s documentation states that its contracts are not audited. PAR’s factory owner can change fees for future launches and can move a creator-fee recipient through PAR’s Community Takeover process. The vault fee destinations depend on PAR’s operators: holder payouts are sent by PAR’s distributor wallet, burn-vault buyback rounds are run by PAR’s operator, and the floor vault’s source code has not been published. SyncNet reads PAR’s parameters live and re-checks them before each launch, but cannot guarantee PAR’s behaviour. The Project Marketplace is non-custodial: SyncNet never holds, escrows or forwards funds. A completed deal transfers the SyncNet operator record (Project Passport) and only the items the listing explicitly names. ETH payments are direct wallet-to-wallet transfers that cannot be reversed; off-chain items depend entirely on the counterparty honouring them, and SyncNet cannot verify or enforce them. Deal only with counterparties you have independently assessed.
