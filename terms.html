Terms of Use

Last updated: 23 September 2026. SyncNet is experimental, non-custodial software. The builder can connect to an EVM wallet, read public chain state, check PAR launch eligibility, prepare multi-market launch calldata and run a simulation. Simulation does not deploy a token.

Real launch execution is available to authorised operators, and to everyone only when this deployment’s server-side rollout gate is opened. A real launch is requested only after a live preflight, a signed intent, an immutable final review, an explicit confirmation and your wallet’s approval. If you approve one, blockchain transactions are irreversible and a successful PAR launch becomes tradable immediately.

“Syncing” a project means selecting one or more assets to form market relationships around that project. A market relationship does not imply endorsement, partnership, ownership or official affiliation between creators or token teams.

SyncNet does not custody private keys or seed phrases and does not guarantee token value, liquidity, fee distributions, rewards, returns, users, holders, community growth or continued availability of any third-party service.

Registry labels say what was checked: “BUILT WITH SYNCNET · VERIFIED” means a launch proof was verified against the chain when the page loaded; other labels (SYNCNET ORIGIN, OPERATOR VERIFIED, PAR INDEXED, PROFILE ONLY, UNVERIFIED) are defined on the Registry page. No label is a quality, safety or endorsement signal. The Project Marketplace records signed listings, offers, operator transfers and deal settlement checklists on SyncNet’s backend; every record is authenticated by a free wallet signature, never a transaction or token approval. It is non-custodial: SyncNet never holds, escrows or forwards funds, and provides no payment guarantee. A completed deal transfers the SyncNet Project Passport (a recognised-operator record) and only the items the listing explicitly names. Token contracts, token supply, locked PAR liquidity and immutable token metadata are never sold and never move. Creator-fee rights move only through PAR’s own on-chain transfer, sent from the seller’s wallet and verified by re-reading the chain. Off-chain items (domains, repositories, community administration) are settled directly between the parties and must be confirmed by both; SyncNet cannot verify or enforce them, and X accounts are never represented as transferable. Operator history is append-only and prior operators remain permanently visible.
