Measuring the mechanism

Two markets.
One measured price.

$SYNC trades in two quote markets: NET and USDG. Each market implies a value for the same token. When those values disagree, traders can buy $SYNC in the cheaper market and sell it in the more expensive one — activity that tends to bring the prices back in sync.
