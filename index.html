How it works

MAP.
SYNC. RECORD.

01

Map

Search any token and see what it is synced with: its direct markets, the projects that use it as a market, and its neighbours.

02

Sync

Sync a new project with up to five eligible tokens. Each connection is a direct market, opened by the PAR launch.

03

Record

SyncNet commits who launched what, with which connections and why, into the launch itself — provenance that cannot be added later.

To sync a project with a token means opening a direct market between them. It is not a partnership, endorsement, shared ownership or automatic holder reward.
