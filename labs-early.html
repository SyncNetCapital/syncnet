EARLY

I was there when. Support a YouTube creator directly from your wallet. Money goes straight to the creator’s verified wallet. SyncNet only proves that it happened, privately, and lets you make an EARLY card later if you want to.

  • Your wallet pays the creator’s wallet directly. SyncNet never receives or holds funds and takes no fee.
  • Two wallet prompts: sign what you mean, then send the transfer.
  • Everything is private unless you choose to share a card.
