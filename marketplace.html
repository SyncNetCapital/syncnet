What a sale is

OPERATIONAL CONTROL, WITH PROOF.

The recognised-operator record (Project Passport) moves to the buyer through a two-signature transfer, together with every right the listing explicitly includes — each labelled ON-CHAIN VERIFIED, SYNCNET-SIGNED or MANUAL / OFF-CHAIN.

What a sale is not

NOT THE TOKEN. NOT THE POOL.

Token supply, holders’ tokens, locked launch liquidity and immutable token metadata are never sold here and never move. The Passport confers no power the underlying contracts do not provide.

Settlement

NON-CUSTODIAL. NO ESCROW.

SyncNet V1 never holds funds and provides no escrow. ETH payments go wallet-to-wallet and are verified on-chain from the transaction; everything else is confirmed by both parties. A deal is COMPLETED only when its checklist really is.

Operational control ≠ token ownership

A listing must state exactly what transfers and what does not. SyncNet verifies signatures and on-chain facts; it does not verify off-chain promises, and it says so wherever that is the case.

Choose an EVM wallet
