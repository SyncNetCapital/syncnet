How provenance works

DISCOVERY ≠
PROVENANCE.

01

Index

Map indexes observable PAR/on-chain market relationships. An indexed market does not mean the project used SyncNet, was verified, or was endorsed.

02

Record

A Registry record says SyncNet has provenance for the project itself. It does not turn market connections into partnerships.

03

Automate

A SyncNet launch proof links the deployer’s signed intent, the salt in the launch transaction and the token that transaction deployed. Anyone can re-check it; this page does, every time it loads.

SIMULATIONS AND BROWSER RECORDS ARE NEVER REGISTRY PROOF.

A launch record kept in your browser is private evidence. It becomes public only as a proof that verifies on-chain — published by SyncNet’s Registry service or listed by the SyncNet operator — and this page re-checks it every time.

Registry ≠ endorsement

A SyncNet provenance record states how a project relates to SyncNet. It does not claim that any token connected by a market approved, endorsed or partnered with the project.
