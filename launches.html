LAUNCHES.

Every launch attempt made in this browser, resolved from the chain. Records are written before your wallet is asked to send, and they are never deleted. The PAR factory on Robinhood Chain is the authority; the PAR indexer only confirms later.

RECOVER A LAUNCH

Paste a transaction hash, a token address or a wallet address. SyncNet reads the chain (and, for a wallet, the PAR indexer) and matches the result with the records in this browser.

IMPORT / EXPORT EVIDENCE

Move launch records between browsers or keep a backup. Imported evidence stays private here and is re-verified from the chain before anything is shown as verified.

Reading launch records…
NEVER LAUNCH TWICE BECAUSE A PAGE LOST TRACK.

If your wallet showed an error, the page closed or the receipt did not arrive, the launch may still have happened. An unresolved record blocks a second launch of the same ticker from the same wallet until the chain says what happened. Use RE-VERIFY; mark an attempt as not sent only after checking your wallet’s activity.

Your records stay in this browser

Launch records are stored in this browser only. They are private evidence until you export a proof or it is published through the Registry. Clearing site data deletes them — export a backup.
